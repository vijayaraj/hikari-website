01 Introducing Hikari

talk your workout.
see your light.

Hikari is a voice-first workout tracker. Speak your sets the way you'd tell a training partner - Hikari logs the reps, weight, and the session, then turns every session into clear, kind insight.

Tap. Talk. Done. "Bench press, 80 kilos, 8 reps." That's the whole interaction.
See how it works
02Voice workout logging

Just talk. We log.

Tapping numbers between sets breaks flow. Hikari listens, parses, and confirms - exercise, weight, sets, reps. Edit anything with a tap. The dumbbells stay in your hands.

  • Natural phrasing - pounds or kilos, sets or singles.
  • Classify each set: warmup, working, or drop.
  • Powered by modern AI models for accurate transcript parsing.
